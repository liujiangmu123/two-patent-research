## 检索要素
- A 对象：输电塔、角钢塔、桁架、杆件 / transmission tower, lattice, truss member
- B 不确定性：置信度、不确定度、对称、信息增益 / confidence, uncertainty, information gain, next-best-view
- C 结构：有限元、灵敏度、应力比、频率 / finite element, sensitivity
- D 规划：补测、航线、视点、子模、TSP / view planning, submodular, coverage path
- E 安全：带电、安全距离、DL/T 409 / live line, safety distance
