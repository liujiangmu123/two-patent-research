ZENMUSE L3 
   
 
See Through, Far and True 
 
DJI's next-generation high-accuracy aerial LiDAR system features a long-range 
LiDAR capable of reaching up to 950 m even on objects with just 10% reflectivity. 
 
Dual 100MP RGB mapping cameras and a high-precision POS system accelerate 
geospatial data acquisition, enabling daily coverage of up to 100 km².
